# 行为准则 · Code of Conduct

本准则适用于本仓库的 issue、Pull Request、讨论、代码评审以及项目相关的其他公开场合。

## 我们的承诺 · Our Pledge

**中文**：我们承诺为所有参与者提供无骚扰的协作环境，不因年龄、体型、可见或不可见的残障、族裔、性别认同与表达、经验水平、教育程度、社会经济地位、国籍、个人外貌、种族、宗教或性取向而区别对待。我们以建设性的方式沟通，共同维护专业、友善、包容的社区。

**English**: We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation. We communicate constructively and keep the community professional, friendly and inclusive.

## 期望行为 · Expected Behavior

**中文**：

- 使用欢迎、包容的语言，尊重不同观点与经验；
- 给出具体、可执行的反馈，就事论事；
- 优雅地接受建设性批评，也愿意修正自己的错误；
- 关注对整个社区最有利的事情；
- 对其他社区成员保持同理心。

**English**:

- Use welcoming and inclusive language and respect differing viewpoints and experiences;
- Give specific, actionable feedback and focus on the work, not the person;
- Gracefully accept constructive criticism and be willing to correct mistakes;
- Focus on what is best for the community;
- Show empathy toward other community members.

## 不可接受行为 · Unacceptable Behavior

**中文**：

- 性化的语言或图像，以及任何形式的性骚扰；
- 挑衅、侮辱或贬损性评论，人身攻击或政治攻击；
- 公开或私下的骚扰；
- 未经明确许可公布他人的隐私信息（如真实姓名、住址、邮箱、内网 IP、截图中的个人信息）；
- 其他在专业环境中明显不恰当的行为。

**English**:

- Sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind;
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks;
- Public or private harassment;
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission;
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## 举报与处理 · Reporting & Enforcement

**中文**：如遭遇或目睹违反本准则的行为，请发送邮件至 **mashuo2010az@163.com**（与安全报告同一渠道）。所有投诉都会被及时、公正地审查，并尽可能保护举报者隐私。维护者有删除、编辑或拒绝不符合本准则的评论、commit、代码、issue 与其他贡献的权利；处理方式视情节包括提醒、警告、临时限制或永久禁止参与。对处理结果有异议可回复同一邮箱说明。**请勿在公开渠道披露举报细节。**

**English**: If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please email **mashuo2010az@163.com** (the same channel as security reports). All complaints will be reviewed promptly and fairly, with the reporter's privacy respected as far as possible. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not align with this Code, and may respond with a reminder, warning, temporary restriction or permanent ban depending on severity. Appeals can be sent to the same address. **Please do not disclose report details publicly.**
